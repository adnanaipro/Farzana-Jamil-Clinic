Privacy notice

NexaCare Medical is not a real clinic. This static demonstration collects no patient information, has no forms or submissions, and does not create a booking, care, or clinic record.

Data and services

The demo uses no analytics, no advertising pixels, marketing cookies, external fonts, review widgets, or third-party scripts. It makes no initial map load and has no real address: the location graphic is local artwork only.

The only optional external contact is a visitor-initiated WhatsApp link to Bukhari AI Solutions. Selecting it opens WhatsApp with a sales-intent message; no message or contact data is sent until the visitor chooses to continue there.

Representative content

Images are AI-generated representative imagery. They do not depict a real clinician, patient, clinic, or consultation. The names, service examples, and presentation are for this demonstration only.

For the one local acknowledgement stored by this site, see the cookie policy. This notice describes the demo, not the privacy practices of any future clinic website.
